# Three-phase upgrade research — checked 1 October 2026

For the screenshot question, lead with the directly local example: **£3,000–£8,000 indicative total upgrade budget**, with the contractor's specific **upgrade-plus-new-board package from £4,995**. Confirm the itemised package scope and separate broker fee. VAT is not stated. This is a London contractor guide, not a market-wide lowest/highest TPI fee. Exact broker arrangement fees remain unpublished in the eight-broker evidence in `tools/tpi-research.json`. The compatible JSON record is `londonEstimate`.

As secondary commercial context only, the £3,500–£9,000 budget is our arithmetic: [Oleco's July 2026 UK commercial guide](https://www.oleco.co.uk/blog/three-phase-power-explained-uk-business) lists £1,500–£4,000 straightforward DNO works and £2,000–£5,000 small-unit internal works. Their endpoints sum to £3,500–£9,000. It is a UK guide proxy for London, with no invented London premium. Metering, broker fees and major reinforcement are outside that sum. VAT is not stated.

Oleco also lists **£5,000–£25,000+ for complex network works alone**. Internal works and fees are additional. The upper value is explicitly open-ended, so it cannot be called a maximum.

[UKPN's current official upgrade cost page](https://www.ukpowernetworks.co.uk/i-already-have-electricity-domestic/adding-more-power/cost-and-time) no longer exposes a numeric generic band in its text. It directs customers to its interactive cost indicator and then a project-specific assessment. UKPN's [scope page](https://www.ukpowernetworks.co.uk/i-already-have-electricity-domestic/adding-more-power/what-do-you-need-to-upgrade) covers homes and small businesses upgrading to three-phase up to 70kVA. Do not attribute the Oleco figures to UKPN as official charges.

A directly local corroboration is [Electrician247.London's advertised three-phase service](https://electrician247.london/three-phase-supply-london/): single-to-three-phase upgrade plus new board **from £4,995**, described as DNO application, excavation, cable, new metering and TP&N board. Its narrative estimates £3,000–£8,000 total, which is not identical to the priced package; retain the starting-price wording and obtain an itemised quote. VAT is not stated. This is a contractor's example, not a TPI fee or an audited London average.

Avoid carrying the £0 claim from [Octopus' domestic upgrade page](https://octopus.energy/upgrades-and-downgrades/) into commercial answers: the page explicitly directs business customers to its business team. Its standard metering statement does not establish a free business upgrade or a zero broker fee.

The three JSON budget objects can be used in the source dialogs and research context. The answer should distinguish an estimate, its stated assumptions, unknown fees and each clickable source, even when a live web search fails.
