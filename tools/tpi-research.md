# Broker comparison research

Checked online: **1 October 2026**. Structured evidence is in `tools/tpi-research.json`.

The JSON contains eight broker/intermediary comparisons and seven London budgeting scenarios. Every record carries clickable source metadata, price scope, VAT basis and a checked date. The same object can support expandable context in the site and evidence in the ask box.

Integration rules:

- Keep broker comparison cards distinct from the existing network price rows.
- Treat `price: Siteworks fee not publicly published` as a missing public tariff, never £0.
- The commission examples are contract illustrations, not install prices or market averages.
- London budget records estimate a published work component or explicitly identified UK guide proxy. They cannot be assigned to a named broker as that broker's quote.
- `basis` explains the calculation; `context` carries exclusions and limitations. Both should appear when a user clicks the price.
- Use each record's VAT wording. SGN's installation/removal tables do not explicitly label VAT; Utility Bidder's guide range does not say whether VAT is included.
- Keep the North London home connection and alteration examples domestic only.
- The SGN Southern examples require the appointed meter owner to be confirmed. Network geography does not establish ownership of an individual meter.
- The UKPN disconnection band covers its region; it is not a London-only statistic.

No site source, relay or build artifact was edited for this research. No credentials were handled and no paid research source was used.
