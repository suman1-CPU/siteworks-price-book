# Siteworks desk dictionary and quote briefs

Checked 2 October 2026. `siteworks-glossary.json` is the canonical content: 60 searchable terms and seven practical quote-preparation checklists. Every term has aliases, a plain-English definition, why it matters, a quote check and primary-source URLs.

The reference helps a Great Britain energy TPI worker specify jobs and understand provider offers. It does not set engineering standards, universal fees, legal caps or guaranteed delivery dates. Checklist items are a practical synthesis of provider guidance; follow the actual provider’s project requirements and offer. Northern Ireland market identifiers and arrangements need separate confirmation.

The electricity upgrade checklist uses the existing book’s `Meter upgrade` job label for compatibility; its content separates network capacity work from meter work. NGN’s 2011 document is used solely for nominal gas-meter terminology, never current prices or regulatory caps. Technical documents support terminology and scope checks, not a substitute for project design. VAT entries deliberately give no blanket percentage; unstated source VAT remains unknown. EDF’s MHHS guidance supports recognition of legacy and updated metering-service names.

## Dictionary index

### Networks and roles

Third-party intermediary (TPI), Distribution Network Operator (DNO), Independent Distribution Network Operator (IDNO), Independent Connection Provider (ICP), Gas distribution network (GDN), Energy supplier, Meter owner, Meter Operator (MOP), Gas Safe registered engineer.

### Supply identifiers

Meter Point Administration Number (MPAN), Meter Point Reference Number (MPRN), Meter serial number (MSN).

### Gas meters and capacity

U6 gas meter, U16 gas meter, U25 gas meter, U40 gas meter, U65 gas meter, U100 gas meter, U160 gas meter, Standard cubic metres per hour (scmh), Gas installation pressure, Meter housing.

### Electricity capacity and metering

Ampere (A), Kilovolt-ampere (kVA), Kilowatt (kW), Kilowatt-hour (kWh), Power factor, Single-phase supply, Three-phase supply, Single-to-three-phase conversion, Supply capacity increase, Network reinforcement, Current-transformer (CT) metering, Half-hourly (HH) data, Smart and advanced (AMR) metering, Agreed supply capacity, Service head / cut-out.

### Works and job scope

New connection, Meter removal, Permanent disconnection, Temporary isolation, Re-energisation, Service alteration / move, Aborted visit.

### Civils and land

Customer excavation, Highway / street works, Civil works, Reinstatement, Wayleave, Easement / servitude, Contestable works, Non-contestable works.

### Quotes and costing

Planning estimate, Quotation, Energy-contract commission, One-off siteworks arrangement fee, Markup, Gross margin, VAT basis, Net and gross price.

## Practical checklist index

Full checklist items are in the JSON for direct use in the site. Each has compatible job labels and its own primary-source references.

- **Electricity upgrade quote brief** (`electric-upgrade`): Meter upgrade, New connection.
- **Gas meter work quote brief** (`gas-meter`): Meter install, Meter upgrade, Meter removal.
- **Gas connection or alteration brief** (`gas-connection`): New connection, Alteration / move.
- **Permanent disconnection brief** (`disconnect`): Disconnection, Meter removal.
- **Civils and land scope check** (`civils`): New connection, Alteration / move, Disconnection, Meter upgrade.
- **Customer quote review** (`customer-quote`): New connection, Meter install, Meter upgrade, Meter removal, Re-energise / isolate, Disconnection, Alteration / move, Aborted visit.
- **Visit readiness and cancellation** (`visit-ready`): Meter install, Meter upgrade, Meter removal, Re-energise / isolate, Disconnection, Alteration / move, Aborted visit.
