    Evidence guide & original price lists

    How to read a card

    CurrentRead from the network's live price list on the check date.
    Older listNewest document found is from 2024 or 2025. The live price is probably higher.
    UnconfirmedSeen in a search result. The document could not be opened to check it.
    No fixed priceNobody publishes one. You get a quote or use the network's online tool.
    • Network standard prices cover one home-sized job that meets the network's rules. Commercial connections are quoted job by job.
    • Meter prices are what the meter owner charges the energy supplier. The supplier's price to the customer is usually higher.
    • Not published anywhere we could find: electricity re-energisation and electricity meter upgrade prices. Ask the supplier.
    • Prices move about once a year, mostly in April. This page does not update itself.

    Where to find the price lists

    Gas pipes

    • Cadent price tools: new connection, alteration, disconnection
    • Northern Gas Networks, April 2026
    • Wales & West Utilities, March 2025
    • SGN connections, April 2024

    Gas meters

    • SGN Southern metering, June 2026
    • SGN Scotland metering, 2026
    • Other networks: search [network] metering charges statement

    Electricity

    • UK Power Networks: cost tool, disconnection costs
    • National Grid South West, April 2026
    • SP Distribution switch-off and switch-on charges, April 2026
    • Others: search [network] statement of methodology and charges for connection

    Suppliers

    • Search [supplier] metering charges pdf or [supplier] non-standard charges
    • Metering charges depend on provider and contract. Request a current written rate card and any broker-specific terms from the supplier or meter operator.
